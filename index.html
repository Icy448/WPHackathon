BankSmart

Your AI assistant for business banking — it watches your cash flow and keeps money in the right place across your own accounts, automatically.
📈

Cash-flow forecast

Projects your balance weeks ahead and flags a shortfall before it happens — like the week three supplier bills and payroll collide.

⟳

Smart sweeps

Moves surplus between your own accounts to pre-fund upcoming bills and earn on idle cash. Internal only — money never leaves your business.

🧾

Payment insights

Spots duplicate invoices, price creep from suppliers, and unusual outgoing payments across all your accounts.

🛡️

Fraud & anomaly alerts
